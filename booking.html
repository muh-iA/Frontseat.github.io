Before You Book

A few common questions.

Where do trips start from?

Almost all trips start with pick-up in Nairobi, where our operations team is based, unless you're already at the coast or another region.

Can packages be customised?

Yes. Every package on our Tours & Packages page can be adjusted for dates, group size, and accommodation level.

How do I pay for a trip?

Once your itinerary is confirmed, our team will share payment details directly — nothing is charged through this website.

What should I pack?

We send a packing list specific to your trip type — safari, hiking, or beach — once your booking is confirmed.
